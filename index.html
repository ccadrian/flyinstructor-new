Process

What to expect

No mystery, no surprises. Here’s exactly how we get from your first flight to your certificate.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We talk through your goal, your timeline, and which rating fits, no pressure, no obligation.

  2. 02

    Ground school

    Airspace, navigation, weather and more, taught in plain language, always tied back to the flying.

  3. 03

    Flight training

    Regular flights with direct, honest feedback after every single one.

  4. 04

    Checkride prep

    Focused, deliberate preparation for both the written and the practical exam.

  5. 05

    License in hand

    You’re holding your certificate, and you decide where the flying takes you next.
